Bethel AMS illustration assets

The user supplied the visual references for this redesign and requested the same
artwork. These files contain only the isolated illustrations: children and church,
gift boxes, books, student avatar, and attendance status illustrations. The source
screen captures and their student records are not included in the deployment.

Illustrations are decorative; names, totals, attendance, rewards, forms, navigation,
and status indicators are rendered by the live application.

Nunito Sans is self-hosted under public/fonts, with its SIL Open Font License.
